Contacts Of Natural Resources

1. Human Use Contacts

  • Used for food and agriculture
  • Used for housing and construction
  • Used for transportation (oil, gas)
  • Used for clothing (cotton, wool)
  • Used for medicine (herbal plants)

2. Industrial Contacts

  • Factories use minerals such as iron, copper, and coal
  • Water is used for power generation and cooling
  • Timber is used for furniture and paper industry
  • Natural gas is used to make chemicals

3. Environmental Contacts

  • Forests interact with the atmosphere
  • Water bodies connect to the water cycle
  • Soil supports plant and animal life
  • Oceans help regulate climate

4. Economic Contacts

  • Natural resources create jobs (farming, mining, fishing)
  • Countries earn money by exporting natural resources
  • Tourism develops from natural beauty

5. Social & Cultural Contacts

  • Nature supports cultural traditions
  • Provides places for recreation
  • Used for spiritual and religious activities
